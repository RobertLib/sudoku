Sudoku that teaches you how to solve it — and never gets in your way. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no internet: every puzzle, every hint and every feature is there from the first launch, and nothing interrupts a game.

HINTS THAT TEACH, NOT JUST TELL
Stuck? A hint first points you in the right direction and lets you find the move yourself. Tap Show Me and it explains the exact deduction step by step: the cells it is about are highlighted, the candidates that matter are colored and chains are drawn on the board. 24 techniques, from Hidden Singles and Naked Pairs to X-Wing, Swordfish, XY-Wing, Unique Rectangles and XY-Chains. Hints also point out a wrong number, or notes that no longer hold the right digit, before they send you any further.

FIVE HONEST LEVELS
Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Master are graded by the techniques a person needs to solve the puzzle, not just by how many numbers are given. Every Medium puzzle really needs a Medium technique, and every Expert puzzle an Expert one. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone — no guessing — and new ones are made on your device for as long as you keep playing.

A DAILY CHALLENGE
• A new puzzle every day, the same one for everyone, getting harder through the week
• Play each day's puzzle on the day to grow your streak, and catch up on missed days in a calendar of the last two years

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Pencil marks and auto notes — a number you place clears itself from the notes in its row, column and box
• Unlimited undo and redo
• Cell first or number first — long-press a number to lock it, then tap every cell where it belongs
• Instant mistake checking with an optional limit of three mistakes and one second chance, rule conflicts only, or no checking at all
• Hide the timer, highlight the row, column and box, see how many of each number are left

A PUZZLE FROM THE NEWSPAPER?
Type it in or paste it. The app checks that it has exactly one solution and tells you how hard it is — and when you get stuck, the hints work on it too, as long as it needs nothing beyond their 24 techniques.

STATISTICS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
Games played, win rate, best and average times, perfect games and streaks for every level, a chart of your solve times and your recent games — plus 22 achievements to unlock. Best and average times only count puzzles solved without hints.

MAKE IT YOURS
Light, dark or system appearance, 8 accent colors and 4 number fonts. Sound effects and haptics, a wave across the board for every finished row, column and box, and confetti for the last number. Leave mid-game and the puzzle is waiting exactly where you left it.

PHONE, TABLET AND KEYBOARD
Portrait and landscape on iPhone and iPad, and keyboard shortcuts with a hardware keyboard: arrows to move, numbers to fill, N for notes, H for a hint, ⌘Z to undo.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
VoiceOver reads every cell and every control, so a whole game can be played by ear. Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion are supported; around the board, text grows up to the first accessibility size.

FAIR PLAY
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions
• No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no data collected
• Works completely offline — there is no networking code in the app
• English and Czech, iPhone and iPad
