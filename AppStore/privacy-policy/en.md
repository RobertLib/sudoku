# Privacy Policy — Sudoku

*Last updated: October 31, 2026*

## In short

Sudoku does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## In detail

**We collect no data.** The app never asks for your name, email address, phone
number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

**It does not connect to the internet.** Sudoku contains no networking code.
Every puzzle — the daily challenge included — is generated on your device, the
hints are worked out on your device, and the sounds are generated at launch, so
everything works entirely offline.

**No analytics, no advertising.** The app contains no ad networks, no analytics
frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere. It
links only Apple's own frameworks.

**No in-app purchases.** The app contains no in-app purchases and no
subscriptions. Nothing in it is for sale, and no feature — the hints included —
is limited by a quota or held back for payment.

**Where your progress is stored.** Your settings, your unfinished games, your
results and statistics, your daily challenge results and your achievements are
stored solely on your device, in the app's own files and the system UserDefaults
store. A few puzzles generated in advance, so a new game starts instantly, are
kept in the app's cache. None of this ever leaves the device. Your progress is
included in your regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have one
enabled, in which case Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app removes this
data from your device.

**The clipboard.** You can paste a puzzle into the Custom Puzzle screen. The app
reads the clipboard only when you tap its Paste button, through the system's own
paste control, and only to read the puzzle from it. It never reads the clipboard
on its own.

**Device access.** The app does not use the camera, microphone, location
services, contacts, photos, health data or any other sensitive permission. It
only uses haptic feedback and audio playback.

**Children.** The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for any age. Because the app
collects no data, no data about anyone of any age is created.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
